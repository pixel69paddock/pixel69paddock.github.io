    How it worksHow to race, odds & points

    Free to enter. There's no betting and no money involved.

    01

    Like or follow

    Follow @dailyderby_horse and you race every day, automatically. Like any reel and you're in every race for the next 2 days. Your @name goes on your horse's tag.

    02

    Comment

    Comment anything on the latest reel. The earlier you comment, the better your place in a tie.

    03

    Get likes

    Likes on your comment raise your odds. Even the favourite usually loses, so anyone can win.

    04

    Claim a look

    Build your horse above and comment its code. Custom horses get a +5–10% boost to their win chance in every race, rolled fresh each race.
