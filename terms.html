Shop terms & refunds

Last updated 25 November 2026. Daily Derby (@dailyderby_horse) is a fan-made pixel horse race on Instagram. Racing is free. The shop sells optional tips and digital cosmetic items.

What you're buying

  • Tips are a thank-you payment to support the game. You get nothing in return beyond our thanks.
  • Skins are digital, cosmetic decorations for one horse, chosen by Instagram handle at checkout. Limited skins are numbered and only that many are sold.
  • Cosmetic only. Nothing you buy changes odds, speed, points or race results. Daily Derby is not betting or gambling, and there are no cash prizes.

Payment

Payments are handled by Stripe. Prices are set in US dollars; Stripe may show and charge them in your own currency, at the rate shown at checkout. We never see your card details. Stripe emails your receipt.

Delivery

A skin is added to the horse of the handle you entered as soon as Stripe confirms the payment, usually within a minute. If you typed someone else's handle, it's a gift to them. Skins appear on this site; they appear in race reels once we've added them to the race art.

Refunds

  • If a limited skin sells out while you're paying, you're refunded in full automatically.
  • If you typed the wrong handle, paid twice, or something didn't arrive, message us within 14 days and we'll fix it or refund you.
  • Otherwise, because digital items are delivered straight away, we don't offer refunds for change of mind. This doesn't limit any rights you have under consumer law where you live, including the Australian Consumer Law.

Under 18?

Please ask a parent or guardian before buying anything.

Removal

If you ask us to remove your horse, its skins go with it. You can ask for a refund of a skin bought in the last 14 days at the same time.

Contact

Message @dailyderby_horse on Instagram, or reply to your Stripe receipt.
